Your phone's music, podcasts, custom actions and active media controls on the watch. The start screen shows only the categories that currently have content, and opens directly when only one is available.

- **Music:** a smooth song list (crown or drag) and a media-control player with the artist, song, previous / play / next, a progress ring and a soft animated background based on the album's colours.
- **Podcasts:** every podcast you added in Watch Menu 2 is a row; a tap opens its episode player, and previous / next moves between episodes.
- **Control media:** control the active Android media session, including supported music and video players, with the same album-art colour treatment when artwork is available.
- **Custom:** run your saved phone-side shortcuts directly from the watch.
- **Volume:** press the speaker button in the player, then turn the crown. Volume control turns itself off after 30 seconds.
- **Save songs:** tap the heart in the player to save the song that's playing, from any app. It turns red whenever a saved song comes on. Tap it again to unsave.
- **Skip 5 seconds:** double-tap the left or right side of the player, away from the buttons, to jump back or forward 5 seconds.
- **Back:** long-press anywhere on the player.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware), the [App Loader](/firmware) (ABI 1.3 or newer), and the **Watch Menu 2** companion app on your Android phone.
